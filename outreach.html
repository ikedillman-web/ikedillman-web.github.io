How this tool uses the API

• Venue Research (web search off) — Claude uses its training knowledge to suggest nightclubs, bars, and lounges in your target city. Fast and free beyond standard token cost, but venues may not be current.

• Venue Research (web search on) — Claude performs at most 2 web searches to find a full batch of real venues. Costs ~$0.02–0.05 per batch search. For deeper contact details on a specific venue, use the Verify button on any card — that fires one targeted search for that venue only (~$0.01).

• Outreach Email — Claude generates a personalized pitch for each venue, matching your DJ profile to their specific vibe and format.

• Model — Currently using claude-sonnet-4-6. To update the model, change the CLAUDE_MODEL constant at the top of this file's script.

• Cost — Each venue search uses roughly 2,000–4,000 tokens (~$0.01–0.02 at current Claude Sonnet pricing). Very affordable.
